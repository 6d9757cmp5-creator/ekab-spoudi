3d-anatomy - licence of the work as distributed
================================================

SUMMARY
-------

This work is a mix of components carrying different upstream licences. When they
are combined and distributed together, the most restrictive terms propagate to
the whole. The effective terms of this distribution are therefore:

  Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/

In practice:

  * NON-COMMERCIAL. This deployment, and any redistribution of it as a whole,
    must not be primarily intended for or directed toward commercial advantage
    or monetary compensation.
  * ATTRIBUTION. Every source listed below must be credited. The required
    attribution strings are collected verbatim in the NOTICE file next to this
    one, and the upstream notice is shipped unmodified as
    public/models/License.txt.
  * SHARE-ALIKE. Derivative works must be distributed under the same terms.

Where the non-commercial clause comes from is stated explicitly in the
"Non-commercial clause" section below, because it comes from two components
only.

NON-COMMERCIAL CLAUSE
---------------------

Only two components carry a NonCommercial restriction:

  * "Anatomy of the Inner Ear" (cochlea and vestibule, nervous.glb) - CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
  * "Kidney" by lissiecowley (visceral.glb) - CC BY-NC 4.0

Everything else in public/models/ is share-alike but commercially usable
(CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan, CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY 4.0). The owner of this project has
decided to keep every structure, including those two, and therefore to publish
the whole work as non-commercial.

If those two components were removed, the remainder could be redistributed
under CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution unchanged. That statement describes the
intent of the licence stack, not legal advice: check the upstream terms
yourself before any commercial use, and re-verify the contents of the .glb
files, since the affected meshes are embedded inside larger files rather than
shipped separately.
