3d-anatomy - required attributions
==================================

This file collects the attribution strings the upstream licences require. Keep
it with any copy or derivative of this work, together with LICENSE and with the
upstream notice shipped at public/models/License.txt.

The work as distributed is non-commercial (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). See LICENSE for
which components impose that restriction.


1. REQUIRED VERBATIM ATTRIBUTIONS
---------------------------------

public/models/License.txt asks that content copied or derived from the models
be attributed as follows. Lines 9 and 11 of that file, quoted exactly:

    -"BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan"

    -"Z-Anatomy - The open source atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0"

Both strings must appear wherever the models, or renderings derived from them,
are redistributed.
