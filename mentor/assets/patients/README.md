# MENTOR NEXT · CC0 patient models (visual use only)

Three third-party static, textured GLB human figures were obtained from the public Innerscene library and are **CC0**, free to use and redistribute. Downloaded via the library's documented Download GLB action and verified as glTF 2.0 GLBs. They are static poses, not rigged animations.

- `alex-standing.glb` — https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/looking-upward-3d-person-standing-fbf49423
- `daniel-lying.glb` — https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/daniel-3d-person-lying-flat-820f78f4
- `amara-sitting.glb` — https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/amara-3d-person-sitting-up-in-bed-3427c39e

The renderer bundled in `mentor/vendor/model-viewer.min.js` is Google model-viewer **v4.3.1, Apache-2.0**, see `mentor/vendor/MODEL-VIEWER-LICENSE.txt` and https://github.com/google/model-viewer .

Do not label these figures as a physiologically responsive patient, injury visualization, anatomically accurate diagnosis or treatment simulation. The visual poses intentionally do **not** change the simulated vitals, dialogue or scores.
